# hackyboard_case

A really simple yet probably good case for my Cherry switches HE keyboard PCB design - hackyboard. 

Everything can be 3d printed(the switch plate should optimally be made out of aluminum tho). It consists of 3 parts + switch plate and can be assembled with m2 heat inserts and screws.

In theory i should also be able to put gasket on top of the switch plate(which is optional), but time will tell

I also tried to make it all compatible with other PCB designs!

It's far from perfection and i migh do v2 of this case in the future!
